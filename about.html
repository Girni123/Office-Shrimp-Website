About me

DAVID

Girnstein / Creator

I am a Creative Head and always up for new possibilities and ideas. Personally in Love with Design.

From Problem to Solution – No Detours.

Office Shrimp was born for a simple reason: I wanted this app for myself. I realized I was sitting like a shrimp way too often, but none of the existing apps actually felt fun to use.

Instead of searching for the perfect solution, I didn't hesitate — I just built it. Driven by the joy of coding and the desire for better posture, Office Shrimp came to life.

A passion project built for my back, and now for yours too.
